# Lutheran Daily Readings

Lutheran Daily Readings is a free app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. It gives you the Scripture readings appointed for each day of the church year, following the historic lectionary of *The Lutheran Hymnal* (1941), along with a family devotion for every day.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which readings does the app use?

- **Sundays and festivals** use the historic propers from *The Lutheran Hymnal*: a First Reading, Psalm, Epistle, and Gospel.
- **Weekdays** follow *The Lutheran Hymnal*'s daily lectionary, with a first and second reading.

The app works out Easter, the moveable seasons, and fixed festivals for you. When a minor festival falls on the same date as a Sunday, the app tells you so beneath that day's readings.

### Where does the daily devotion come from?

From *Book of Family Prayer* by Bishop N. J. Laache. Each day includes an opening prayer, a Scripture text, a meditation, a closing prayer, and a hymn. Where a recording of the hymn is available, tap **Listen**.

### How do I choose which Bible app or translation opens?

On the **Readings** tab, tap the **Settings** button (the gear), then choose:

- **Translation:** NKJV, ESV, or NIV
- **Bible App:** YouVersion, Olive Tree, Logos, Blue Letter Bible, Bible Gateway, or Bible Hub

Tap any reading to open it there.

### A reading opened in Bible Gateway instead of my Bible app. Why?

If the Bible app you chose isn't installed, or it can't open the passage, the reading opens on the Bible Gateway website instead. Install the app, or choose a different one in Settings.

### How do I set up the daily reminder?

Open **Settings**, turn on **Daily Reminder**, and pick a time. The first time, you'll be asked to allow notifications. Each reminder names that day's readings.

### My reminder isn't arriving.

- Make sure notifications are allowed: open the **Settings** app, tap **Notifications**, then **Lutheran Daily Readings**. If they're turned off, the app's Settings screen shows an **Open Settings** button that takes you there.
- Check that a Focus or Do Not Disturb setting isn't silencing it.
- The app schedules reminders about two months ahead and tops them up whenever you open it. If you haven't opened the app in a couple of months, open it once to resume them.

### What do the "~3 minutes" labels mean?

They're an estimate of how long each reading takes at an average reading pace.

### Why does the color change through the year?

The app marks each season with its traditional liturgical color: blue for Advent, gold for Christmas and Easter, violet for Lent, and green for Epiphany and Trinity.

### Does the app need an internet connection?

No. All the readings and devotions are built into the app. You only need a connection to open a passage on a website or to listen to a hymn.

## Privacy policy

*Last updated: October 8, 2026*

Lutheran Daily Readings does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

- **No data collection.** The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking, and it doesn't send any information about you or your use of the app to the developer or anyone else.
- **Settings stay on your device.** Your choices of translation, Bible app, and reminder time are saved only on your device.
- **Reminders are local.** Daily reminders are scheduled by your device. No push notification service is used.
- **Links to other apps and websites.** When you open a reading or a hymn recording, the app hands that link to another app or website (such as YouVersion or Bible Gateway). Those services have their own privacy policies.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Questions about privacy can be sent to [objects.36.scuba@icloud.com](mailto:objects.36.scuba@icloud.com).
